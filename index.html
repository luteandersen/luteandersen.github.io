Books

Philosophy · Essay

God Is Dead. Now What?

The Meaning of Life, the Individual, and Humanity

A philosophical essay about what remains after God disappears as the central explanation: biology, consciousness, individual purpose, and the possible direction of humanity. Ambitious questions, kept close enough to the ground to avoid floating away.

Science Fiction · Absurdity

Jovian Chronicles, Vol. 1

A collection of stories centred on giant planets, failed missions, scientific absurdity, cosmic bureaucracy and the profound insignificance of humanity. Serious space imagery meets dry humour, existential paperwork, and machines that may or may not understand the assignment.

Support the work

The books are free. Your support is optional.

I make the complete books available for free. If you enjoy them and want to contribute financially, the simplest way is to buy a copy on Amazon.

You can also support the work without spending anything: subscribe to the channels and share the books, videos or stories with someone who might enjoy them. Helping the work reach the right people genuinely matters.

Thank you. Every purchase helps me dedicate more time to writing, researching and creating new work. At the moment, I build these projects during the limited free hours left by a normal job. If they ever earn enough to support themselves, I could give them many more hours instead of only the few I can spare now.
